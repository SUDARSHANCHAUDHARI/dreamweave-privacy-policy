# DreamWeave Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 21, 2026
_Last updated: March 21, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

DreamWeave ("we," "our," or "us") is a private dream journal app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and your choices. By using DreamWeave you agree to the practices described below.

## Information We Collect

### Journal Data (Stored Locally)
- **Dream entries**: Text, mood tags, and timestamps you write.
- **Voice memos**: Audio recordings attached to dream entries.
- All journal and audio data is stored exclusively on your device using Room database.

### AI Analysis (Optional)
- If you choose to request AI-powered dream analysis, the text of your dream entry is sent to **Google Gemini API**.
- Audio is never sent to any server — only text you explicitly submit for analysis.
- See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Gemini data practices.

### No Account Required
- DreamWeave does not require sign-in and does not collect your name, email, or any account information.

## How We Use Your Information

- Display your journal entries within the app.
- Generate AI dream analysis when you explicitly request it.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

## Storage and Retention

- All dream entries and voice memos are stored locally on your device.
- Data is retained until you delete individual entries or uninstall the app.
- No data is backed up to any SudarshanTechLabs server.

## Data Security

- Local data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption.
- Text sent to Gemini API is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.

## Permissions Used

- **RECORD_AUDIO**: Required to record voice memos attached to dream entries.
- **READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: Required to save and read voice memo files.
- **INTERNET**: Required only when you request AI dream analysis via Gemini.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Delete any entry at any time from within the app.
- Disable AI analysis by simply not using that feature — no text is ever sent automatically.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

## Children's Privacy

DreamWeave is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revisions. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
